CellMap2 Yeast Higher-Order Complex Catalog

AlphaFold3 multimer predictions (3–8 chains) for candidate higher-order assemblies extended from the confidence-filtered S. cerevisiae CellMap 2.0 pairwise interactome (52,937 predicted pairs among 5,354 genes). Candidate complexes were grown from the network's own high-confidence interactions and merged where they overlapped, not picked by model score; the 125 listed here are those whose AlphaFold3 fold formed one fully connected structure with no atomic clash. Connections: chain pairs that form an interface in the AlphaFold3 fold (iLIS ≥ 0.10 in at least one of the 5 models, the same rule used to call a fold fully connected). PDB: the experimental structure holding the most of the complex's subunits, how many it holds, its size and what it lacks (see How to read this table). The cutoff filter uses each complex's iLIS: for each chain pair the best of the 5 models, averaged over chain pairs.

How to read this table
Which complexes are listed — candidate complexes were grown from the network's own high-confidence interactions (no model score involved) and folded with AlphaFold3. Only folds that formed one fully connected structure with no atomic clash are listed; partial, failed or clashing folds, and candidates not yet folded, are not shown.
PDB — the experimental structure (X-ray, cryo-EM or NMR) holding the most of this complex's subunits, at least 2. Each subunit's UniProt accession gives its PDB entries, and a subunit counts only if RCSB lists that protein among the entry's chains. S. cerevisiae entries come first; only when none holds the complex well is a homolog entry shown, through human, mouse or S. pombe orthologs (DIOPT). A lighter ID means partial: under 60% of the subunits, and only 2 of them or inside a much larger assembly. When several entries qualify, a non-partial one comes first, then the one holding the most subunits, then the fewest other proteins, then the best resolution, then the newest. Hover for the title, method and resolution. — = no experimental entry holds 2 or more of the subunits. PDB and UniProt searched on ….
Matched — subunits of this complex in that entry / subunits in the complex.
PDB size — distinct proteins in the entry (RNA and DNA not counted); PDB size minus matched = other proteins in it.
Not in PDB — this complex's subunits the entry lacks.
Species — the organism of the entry.
Structure — opens in LIVIA with all ranked models, per-model scores and PAE maps.
